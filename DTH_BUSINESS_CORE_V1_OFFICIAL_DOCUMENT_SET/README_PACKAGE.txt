Important operating rules
- Accepted != Paid.
- Finance verifies Paid.
- Accepted quotation commercial content is locked; use Revision for changes.
- Quotation items come from valid Price Books.
- Payment Ledger is the V1 verified-money source of truth.
- Receipt is not a VAT/e-invoice.
- Customer/Customer Care begins after Paid.
- Action visibility = Permission + Business Function/Assignment + Record State/Policy.
